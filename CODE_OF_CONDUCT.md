# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The `code-to-docx` community should be safe, welcoming, and fair to everyone who takes part in good faith.

We respect each person's dignity and contribution regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, religion or philosophy, national or social origin, economic position, education, experience level, or other status.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- treat other people with patience and respect;
- discuss ideas honestly without attacking the person behind them;
- respect different backgrounds, viewpoints, and levels of experience;
- give and accept constructive feedback;
- take responsibility for mistakes and make a reasonable effort to repair harm; and
- protect private or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or unwanted personal attention;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or demeaning comments;
- discrimination or stereotyping based on identity or personal characteristics;
- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- sharing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation or evading an enforcement action through a misleading identity;
- spam or commercial promotion unrelated to the project; and
- other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional open source community.

## Reporting a problem

Report a possible violation through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/xllily/code-to-docx/security/advisories/new). Start the report title with `Code of Conduct` so the maintainer can route it correctly.

If private reporting is unavailable, open a public issue without names, screenshots, message excerpts, or other incident details. Ask the maintainer to provide a private contact channel.

The maintainer will review reports as promptly as practical. Reports and enforcement discussions will remain private to the extent possible, subject to safety needs and legal requirements.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate it.

Enforcement depends on the severity and impact of the conduct, whether it has happened before, and whether the person responsible is willing to repair the harm. Possible actions include:

1. A private correction or warning.
2. A temporary limit on participation or contact.
3. A temporary suspension from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban when other measures cannot keep the community safe.

The maintainer may skip steps when an incident is serious enough to require a stronger response.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public, including through an official account or at an event.
